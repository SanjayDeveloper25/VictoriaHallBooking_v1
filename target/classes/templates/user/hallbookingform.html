Victoria Public Hall – Venue Description


Victoria Public Hall (VPH), built in 1887, is Chennai’s historic Town Hall and a landmark under the custodianship of the Greater Chennai Corporation. Restored with sensitivity to its architectural and cultural heritage, VPH now functions as a vibrant civic and cultural venue.

The Hall is dedicated to hosting educational, cultural, intellectual, and heritage-aligned programmes that serve the public interest while preserving the historic fabric of the building.

Facilities Available
300-seater Main Auditorium (with stage, green room, VIP hall, basic lighting & sound)

Suitable For

  • Cultural performances
  • Lectures, talks, and panel discussions
  • Academic and intellectual events
  • Exhibitions and curated displays





Not Permitted

  • Political events
  • Religious events
  • Weddings, receptions, family or private functions
  • Commercial promotions unrelated to culture or public interest

All bookings are subject to approval by the Competent Authority of the Greater Chennai Corporation and must comply with the heritage character and operational guidelines of VPH.

You must submit No Object Certificate (NOC) obtained from the police authorities for your booking to be confirmed. Failure to submit will lead to an automatic cancellation of the event, the day bfeore the event date. Incase of cancellation, only the deposit amount will be refunded and the booking charges will be forfeited.
